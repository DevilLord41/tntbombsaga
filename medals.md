# Medals

<img src="img/items/supreme-king-medal.png" width="64" alt="">

Medals open at **level 50**. You wear one medal in each of the 9 medal slots, and the stats of the medals you wear add up. Medals are crafted from other medals with Coins.

## Slots

Each slot holds one medal of its own type. The first 4 slots are open from the start. The others open when the **total BP of the medals you are wearing** reaches the number below (not your character's BP).

| Slot | Opens at medal BP |
|---|---|
| [Festival Medal](#medal-slot-1) | open from the start |
| [Contest Medal](#medal-slot-2) | open from the start |
| [Guild Medal](#medal-slot-3) | open from the start |
| [Challenge Medal](#medal-slot-4) | open from the start |
| [Royal Medal](#medal-slot-5) | 24,500 |
| [Fighter Medal](#medal-slot-6) | 24,500 |
| [Title Medal](#medal-slot-7) | 113,000 |
| [Peerage Medal](#medal-slot-8) | 113,000 |
| [Supreme Medal](#medal-slot-9) | 300,000 |

## Stats

- **DMG Down** lowers the damage you take by that percent.
- **Burst DMG-** lowers the damage of critical hits you take.
- **BP** is the BP the game shows for the medal: ATK × 3.3 + DEF × 3.3 + HP × 0.5 + Burst DMG- × 3 + DMG Down (each 0.01%) × 6.298, rounded up.

## Crafting

Open **Craft Medal**, pick the medal and press Craft:

- Crafting always succeeds. The medals used are gone; the Coins are the price per medal.
- You can craft up to **99** at once.
- **Auto-craft "yes"**: when you are missing some of the medals it needs, the game offers to craft those first in the same go (their Coins are added to the price).
- A medal you are wearing is not used; take it off first.
- **From scratch** = all the medals that cannot be crafted and all the Coins one medal costs when every step is crafted (with two recipes, the cheaper one).
