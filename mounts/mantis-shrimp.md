# Mantis Shrimp

<img src="../img/mounts/mantis-shrimp.png" width="120" alt="">

Deep-sea bully tamed by a warrior. Adored by people on land, even became a popular meme one time.

## Where to Get

- Chest <img src="../img/items/6-star-mount-selection-chest-1.png" width="20" alt=""> **5-Star Mount Random Chest**: 6.25% chance per chest (chest from: Event)
- Chest <img src="../img/items/6-star-mount-selection-chest-1.png" width="20" alt=""> **5-Star Mount Selection Chest 1**: pick 1 of 9 (chest from: Event)
- Chest <img src="../img/items/year-of-the-tiger-chest.png" width="20" alt=""> **Year of the Tiger Chest**: 3.00% chance per chest (chest from: Event)
- Also check the **Mount** tab of the Store (paid in Mount Coins) and current events in game: their stock is not listed outside the game.

## Skills

Like every mount, Mantis Shrimp has 4 skill slots and can learn any of the 16 [mount skills](../mounts.html#skills). Learning or changing the skill of one slot costs <img src="../img/items/skill-page.png" width="20" alt=""> Skill Page ×1; the skill you get is random.

Tiers go from White to **Orange** for this mount. "To unlock the tier" is paid once (Activate for the first tier, Advance when all 4 pieces are at +10). "Each Upgrade" is the price of one press, which raises one random piece; a tier has 36 piece level-ups (4 pieces from +1 to +10). Stats are the 4 pieces together at +10 of the tier. Riding Points count for your character as long as you own the mount. More: [Mount equipment](../mounts.html#equipment).
