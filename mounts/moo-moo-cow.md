# Moo-moo Cow

<img src="../img/mounts/moo-moo-cow.png" width="120" alt="">

When it was still in the snowfield, it accidentally ate some sprouts in the depth of the Frozen Ruins. After that, it gained intelligence and monstrous strength. It ran away when herdsmen were not looking.

## Where to Get

- Chest <img src="../img/items/6-star-mount-selection-chest-1.png" width="20" alt=""> **6-Star Mount Selection Chest 2**: pick 1 of 8 (chest from: Event)
- Also check the **Mount** tab of the Store (paid in Mount Coins) and current events in game: their stock is not listed outside the game.

## Skills

Like every mount, Moo-moo Cow has 4 skill slots and can learn any of the 16 [mount skills](../mounts.html#skills). Learning or changing the skill of one slot costs <img src="../img/items/skill-page.png" width="20" alt=""> Skill Page ×1; the skill you get is random.
