# Parts

<img src="img/items/armor-piercing.png" width="64" alt=""> 

Parts (Part in the game) are 4 pieces you assemble on your character. Each part gives stats that grow with its enhance level, opens up to 3 extra recast stats, and parts of the same set give set bonuses. Parts come out of Code Cases, which you crack on a console.

## Rules

- Parts open at **level 33**.
- You wear up to **4 parts**, one per seat. Any part can go in any seat, but the same part can't be worn twice ("Duplicate accessories cannot be equipped."). The stats of the parts you wear add up.
- Every part has a type: **Attack**, **Defense**, **Assist**, **Tactics** (the game's other texts and the case names call Assist "Support"). The type is shown on the part and, per the game's text, decides which stats its recasts can roll (see [Recast](#recast)).
- Getting a part you already have (game text): "When obtaining the same part, higher-level parts replace old ones; others are converted to materials." The game doesn't show how many materials a copy gives.
- **Quick Assemble**: when a set's info shows the button (you own enough of its parts to activate another tier), it puts that set's parts on first and fills the remaining seats with the parts you were wearing.

<a id="enhance"></a>
## Enhance

- A part starts at level 1 and can be enhanced up to **level 50**.
- Each try costs the part's own **blueprint** (every part has its own, see the tables below) and Coins. The materials are used up on every try, whether it succeeds or not.
- The success rate falls as the level rises. A success raises the part by one level.
- Each stat at level *n* = base × (1 + (n + 2) × (n − 1) / 40), rounded down (base = the level 1 value). The **Stats ×** column below is that multiplier for the level the try leads to.

<a id="recast"></a>
## Recast

- Besides its base stats, a part has 3 recast stat lines. Line 1 opens at enhance level 10, line 2 at level 20, line 3 at level 30 ("Unlocks after enhanced to Lv.10").
- Recasting a line spends its material and gives that line a new random stat and value; the game shows the value and its maximum.
- Stats a recast can roll, by part type (game text; * = higher values):
  - Attack: ATK\*, CRIT, DMG+, HP
  - Defense: DEF\*, DMG-, TOU, HP
  - Support: END, DEF, LUCK, PEN, HP\*
  - Tactics: ATK, LUCK, PEN\*, DMG+\*, DEF, HP
  - Stats marked with \* have higher values.
- Recast stats vary by enhance level and stat unlock order.

| Line | Opens at | Cost per recast |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lv 10 | <img src="img/items/parts.png" width="20" alt=""> Parts ×1 |
| 2 | Lv 20 | <img src="img/items/coil.png" width="20" alt=""> Coil ×1 |
| 3 | Lv 30 | <img src="img/items/smart-chip.png" width="20" alt=""> Smart Chip ×1 |

Every part uses these same recast materials.

## The parts

- **Lv 1** = the part's base stats, then its stats at higher enhance levels (formula above).
- **Sets** = the sets the part belongs to.
- Every part comes from Code Cases: the second table of each type lists the cases that can hold the part or a stack of its blueprints, with the chance per case.

## Crack: Code Cases

- Parts come out of **Code Cases**. Put a case on a free console to crack it; each console cracks one case at a time, and the case leaves your bag.
- Cracking takes the case's time. When it is done, take the contents. **Unlock Now** ends it at once for 1 Diamond per 40 seconds left (rounded up).
