# Rosefinch(LD)

| Base | Advance I | Advance II |
|---|---|---|
| <img src="../img/pets/rosefinch-ld.png" width="96" alt=""> | <img src="../img/pets/rosefinch-ld-advance-1.png" width="96" alt=""> | <img src="../img/pets/rosefinch-ld-advance-2.png" width="96" alt=""> |

As one of the legendary 4 Mythic Beasts, it could instantly burn anything to ash with its nirvana fire. When a Battle Kingdom god broke its fresh in the War of Gods thousands of years ago, it dropped into a mountain in the south of the kingdom, turning the place into a world of fire. No one has made it into the depth of the Fire Mountain yet.
