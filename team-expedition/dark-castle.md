# Dark Castle

<img src="../img/pve/dark-castle.png" width="160" alt="">

The heroes discover a castle in the dark forest, it must have something to do with the Demon Queen.

- Needs player level **22**.
- 3 difficulties (Easy, Normal, Hard), each with 2 stages.
- HP is shown for the stages where it has been recorded in real battles.
