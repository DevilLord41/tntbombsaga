# Demon Forest

<img src="../img/pve/demon-forest.png" width="160" alt="">

The Demon King's forces have entered the Battle Continent, waiting for the right moment in the woods. We must find out what his plan is and the Demon Queen's whereabouts.

- Needs player level **29**.
- 3 difficulties (Easy, Normal, Hard), each with 2 stages.
- HP is shown for the stages where it has been recorded in real battles.
