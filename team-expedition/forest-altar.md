# Forest Altar

<img src="../img/pve/forest-altar.png" width="160" alt="">

The Demon Queen disappeared close to the Altar, as the ancient Spirits were woken up from all the commotion.

- Needs player level **14**.
- 3 difficulties (Easy, Normal, Hard), each with 2 stages.
- HP is shown for the stages where it has been recorded in real battles.
