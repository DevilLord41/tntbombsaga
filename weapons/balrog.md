## Ultimate: Burning in flames

Throws a giant Molotov Cocktail, dealing massive base damage. Upon impact, it leaves burning flames in the crater, dealing significant base damage each round to enemies within the area for 3 rounds. Ultimate skill does not consume SP.

| Levels | Base DMG | Additional |
|---|---|---|
| S0 - S3 | 140% | Burning flames deal 160% damage to enemies in the area. |
| S4 - S7 | 160% | Burning flames deal 165% damage to enemies in the area. |
| S8 - S11 | 180% | Burning flames deal 170% damage to enemies in the area. |
| S12 - S13 | 200% | Burning flames deal 180% damage to enemies in the area. |
