## Ultimate: Focus Attack

Fires 3 consecutive standard shells, each dealing additional damage.

| Levels | Base DMG | Additional |
|---|---|---|
| S0 - S3 | 100% | Deals an additional 24% damage. |
| S4 - S7 | 100% | Deals an additional 28% damage. |
| S8 - S11 | 100% | Deals an additional 32% damage. |
| S12 - S13 | 100% | Deals an additional 35% damage. |
