## Ultimate: One Shot

Shoots a highly powerful arrow, dealing massive base damage to the target and reducing their ATK for 2 rounds. Does not create a crater.

| Levels | Base DMG | Additional |
|---|---|---|
| S0 - S3 | 260% | Reduces the target's ATK by 5%. |
| S4 - S7 | 270% | Reduces the target's ATK by 8%. |
| S8 - S11 | 280% | Reduces the target's ATK by 10%. |
| S12 - S13 | 300% | Reduces the target's ATK by 10%; if teamed up with spouse, both players gain a 5% ATK increase, and the target's ATK is further reduced by an additional 15%. |
