## Ultimate: Hawkeye

Fires a series of shells, each dealing base damage.

| Levels | Base DMG | Additional |
|---|---|---|
| S0 - S3 | 90% | Fires 2-3 shells in succession. |

| S12 - S13 | 110% | Fires 3-5 shells in succession; if a target is killed, restores 20% HP. |
