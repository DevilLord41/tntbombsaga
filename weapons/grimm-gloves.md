## Ultimate: Heavy Punch

Throws 3 times consecutively, each hit dealing base damage to the target and inflicting Bleed. The target loses HP equal to a certain amount of damage per round for 2 rounds.

| Levels | Base DMG | Additional |
|---|---|---|
| S0 - S3 | 80% | Bleeding deals 30% damage. |
| S4 - S7 | 85% | Bleeding deals 33% damage. |
| S8 - S11 | 90% | Bleeding deals 36% damage. |
| S12 - S13 | 100% | Bleeding deals 40% damage; additionally, there is a 30% chance to trigger a heavy punch glove, stunning the target for 2 rounds. |
