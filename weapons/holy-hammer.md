## Ultimate: Purify Mind

Cleanses all debuffs from yourself and allies, restoring HP for all allies and dealing base damage.

| Levels | Base DMG | Additional |
|---|---|---|
| S0 - S3 | 160% | Restores 5% HP. |
| S4 - S7 | 170% | Restores 6% HP. |
| S8 - S11 | 180% | Restores 7% HP. |
| S12 - S13 | 200% | Restore 8% HP; Activate Archangel Guardian form, purifying each effect grants an additional 5% HP to the caster. |
