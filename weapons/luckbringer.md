## Ultimate: Lucky Star

Throws 3 times, each releasing a series of shells, with each shell dealing base damage to the target.

| Levels | Base DMG | Additional |
|---|---|---|
| S0 - S3 | 50% | Fires 2 shells per attack. |
| S4 - S7 | 53% | Fires 2 shells per attack. |
| S8 - S11 | 56% | Fires 3 shells per attack. |
| S12 - S13 | 60% | Fires 3 shells per attack. If 6 or more shells hit the same target, next round's damage increases by 20%. |
