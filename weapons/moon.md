## Ultimate: Fireworks

Fires 3 shells, each dealing base damage with a 60% increase in splash radius and causing splash damage.

| Levels | Base DMG | Additional |
|---|---|---|
| S0 - S3 | 105% | Splash damage equals 70% of the ultimate skill damage. |
| S4 - S7 | 115% | Splash damage equals 80% of the ultimate skill damage. |
| S8 - S11 | 125% | Splash damage equals 90% of the ultimate skill damage. |
| S12 - S13 | 135% | Splash damage equals 100% of the ultimate skill damage. |
